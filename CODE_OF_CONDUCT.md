
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal circumstances.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behaviour include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness.
- Respecting differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks and insulting comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Other inappropriate or unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behaviour.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately to project maintainers through an appropriate contact channel.

All complaints will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Project maintainers will follow these guidelines when determining consequences:

### 1. Correction
A private warning explaining the inappropriate behaviour and requesting correction.

### 2. Warning
A formal warning for serious or repeated violations.

### 3. Temporary Ban
A temporary suspension from interacting with the project community.

### 4. Permanent Ban
Permanent removal from project participation for severe or continued violations.
